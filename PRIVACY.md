# Privacy & data

Ballistic Calculator does not have accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting. Nothing
about your shooting is uploaded to us.

## Stored on your device

Your setup (cartridge, bullet, sight, scope clicks, zero range and atmosphere), any custom calibers
you add, and the date you accepted the in-app terms are kept in the app's local storage. They are
removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Location

The Weather tab asks for location permission only when you open it, and uses your position solely
to request a forecast. The app does not store your coordinates. You can revoke the permission in
system settings at any time; every other tab keeps working without it.

## Weather requests

Refreshing the weather sends your latitude and longitude (rounded to four decimals) to MET Norway at
api.met.no, along with your IP address as part of any internet request. MET Norway is the only third
party the app contacts. Their handling of those requests is described in their own terms.

## Attribution

Weather data comes from MET Norway (api.met.no) and is licensed under CC BY 4.0. It is modified in
one way: the forecast's sea-level pressure is converted to station pressure for the reported
elevation, which is the value the solver needs.
