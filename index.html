Ballistic Calculator

Long-range ballistics, offline, in your pocket

A G1/G7 trajectory solver with a wind matrix, a load library and live field weather — built for the range and the paddock, with no signal.

Download the latest APK Android 8.0+ · version 1.2.0 · works offline · installs over the previous version

Install

  1. Tap Download the latest APK on your Android phone — the newest version is always the top entry on the releases page. Take the standard Ballistic-Calculator-<version>.apk for a 64-bit phone; the -universal APK is for older 32-bit devices that refuse it.
  2. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installing apps from this source: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect offers an "App scan recommended" step, let the scan finish before retrying — a scan that times out can report "App not installed".

Updates install over the top and keep your saved setups, custom calibers and preferences.

What it does

Trajectory tableDrop, windage, MOA, energy, time of flight and scope clicks out to 1000 m or yards.
Wind matrixDrift and holds across 2–20 m/s (5–45 mph) crosswinds, in cm, inches, MOA or clicks.
G1 and G7The BC's own drag function is used — G7-quoted match bullets solve as G7 — plus shot-angle correction for uphill and downhill fire.
Loads and calibersBundled factory loads and bullets, 150+ cartridges, and your own custom calibers.
Field weatherCurrent conditions from MET Norway fill temperature, station pressure and wind.
Your scopePick a ⅛, ¼, ⅓, ½ or 1 MOA click, or type any value — the tables follow it.
OfflineEverything except the weather tab runs with no connection and saves on the device.
